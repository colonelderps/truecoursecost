Payhip pricing and fees

No affiliate links: every vendor link goes straight to the platform's own pricing page. Disclosure.

Fees change; confirm on the vendor's pricing page before buying.

Calculate your annual cost

Payhip's Free plan has a 5% transaction fee, Plus ($29 a month) has 2%, and Pro ($99 a month) has 0%. Processing is through your own Stripe or PayPal at 2.9% + 30c. Plus pays for itself above about $967 a month in sales, and Pro beats Plus above $3,500.
