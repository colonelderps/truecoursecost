Kajabi vs Skool cost

No affiliate links: every vendor link goes straight to the platform's own pricing page. Disclosure.

Fees change; confirm on the vendor's pricing page before buying.

Calculate your annual cost

Skool Hobby ($9 plus 10% + 30c) costs less than Kajabi Basic ($179, 2.9% + 30c) below about $2,394 a month in sales. Skool Pro ($99, 2.9% + 30c) is $80 a month cheaper than Kajabi Basic on sales under $900, but sales above $900 cost 3.9% + 30c. Skool only processes through its own merchant account.

Skool's help center charges one bundled fee of 10% + 30c per transaction on all sales (https://help.skool.com/article/86-subscriptions-faq). It doesn't split out a platform cut, so the whole fee goes in native_processor and platform_fee_pct is 0. Payments must go through Skool's own merchant account ('you cannot use your existing Stripe account'), so you can't use your own processor; members, courses, videos and live calls are unlimited, and annual price = '2 months free' on the pricing page (monthly x 10/12).
