No affiliate links: every vendor link goes straight to the platform's own pricing page. Disclosure.

Calculate your annual cost

Teachable Starter ($39 plus 7.5%) costs less than Kajabi Basic ($179) below about $1,867 a month in sales. Teachable Builder ($89) has the same 2.9% + 30c processing and is $90 a month cheaper at any revenue. Kajabi Pro's 2.7% would only beat Teachable Growth ($189, 2.9%) above roughly $155,000 a month.
