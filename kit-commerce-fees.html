Kit Commerce fees

No affiliate links: every vendor link goes straight to the platform's own pricing page. Disclosure.

Fees change; confirm on the vendor's pricing page before buying.

Calculate your annual cost

Kit charges a single 3.5% + 30c commerce fee that already includes card processing, so don't add Stripe on top. The fee is the same on the Free ($0), Creator ($33) and Pro ($66) plans, so upgrading doesn't lower it. Subscription prices scale with list size, and we only captured the base tier.
