Gumroad vs Payhip cost

No affiliate links: every vendor link goes straight to the platform's own pricing page. Disclosure.

Fees change; confirm on the vendor's pricing page before buying.

Calculate your annual cost

Per secondary sources for Gumroad, it takes 10% + 50c per sale plus 2.9% + 30c processing, while Payhip's Free plan takes 5% and charges 2.9% + 30c through your own Stripe. Neither has a monthly fee, so Payhip costs less at every price and revenue level: a $10 sale costs about $1.09 on Payhip versus $2.09 on Gumroad. Gumroad is merchant of record for sales tax, and the data doesn't record that for Payhip.
