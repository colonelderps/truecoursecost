Gumroad fees

No affiliate links: every vendor link goes straight to the platform's own pricing page. Disclosure.

Fees change; confirm on the vendor's pricing page before buying.

Calculate your annual cost

Per secondary sources, Gumroad has no monthly fee but takes 10% + 50c per direct sale, or a flat 30% on Discover marketplace sales. Card processing of 2.9% + 30c is charged on top, so a $10 sale costs about $2.09 (20.9%) and a $200 sale about $26.60 (13.3%). Gumroad is merchant of record and handles sales tax, which can suit sellers who want to avoid tax admin.
