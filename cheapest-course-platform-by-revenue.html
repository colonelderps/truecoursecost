Calculate your annual cost

At the lowest revenue, the free plans cost least up front: Systeme.io, Kit, Payhip and Gumroad (Gumroad per secondary sources). As sales grow, percentage fees add up, so flat-price plans with no transaction fee pull ahead. On modelled fees alone, Systeme.io ($0 to $97 a month, 2.9% + 30c processing) is the lowest across the revenue range, but its free plan allows one course and the calculator doesn't model plan limits.
