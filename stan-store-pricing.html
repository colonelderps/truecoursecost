Stan Store pricing

No affiliate links: every vendor link goes straight to the platform's own pricing page. Disclosure.

Fees change; confirm on the vendor's pricing page before buying.

Calculate your annual cost

Stan Store's Creator plan costs $29 a month ($25 billed annually) and Creator Pro costs $99 ($79 annually), with no Stan transaction fee. Payments go through Stripe or PayPal, at 2.9% + 30c for Stripe in the US. It suits sellers who want a low flat price and no revenue share; Creator Pro adds payment plans and order bumps.
