Podia vs Teachable cost

No affiliate links: every vendor link goes straight to the platform's own pricing page. Disclosure.

Fees change; confirm on the vendor's pricing page before buying.

Calculate your annual cost

Teachable Starter ($39 plus 7.5%) costs less than Podia Mover ($49 plus 5%) below about $400 a month in sales, and Mover costs less above that. Teachable Builder ($89, 0%) beats Mover above about $800, and is $10 a month cheaper than Podia Shaker ($99, 0%) at any revenue. Processing is 2.9% + 30c on both.
