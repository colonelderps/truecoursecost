Kajabi pricing

No affiliate links: every vendor link goes straight to the platform's own pricing page. Disclosure.

Fees change; confirm on the vendor's pricing page before buying.

Calculate your annual cost

Kajabi's Basic, Growth and Pro plans cost $179, $249 and $499 a month, with no revenue share. Kajabi Payments charges 2.9%, 2.8% and 2.7% + 30c by plan, and using a third-party gateway adds 2%, 1% or 0.5%. It suits sellers who can comfortably cover a higher flat subscription.
