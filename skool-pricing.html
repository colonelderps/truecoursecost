Skool pricing and transaction fees

No affiliate links: every vendor link goes straight to the platform's own pricing page. Disclosure.

Fees change; confirm on the vendor's pricing page before buying.

Calculate your annual cost

Skool's Hobby plan costs $9 a month with a bundled 10% + 30c fee on sales, and Pro costs $99 a month with 2.9% + 30c (3.9% + 30c on sales above $900). Payments must run through Skool's own merchant account. Hobby suits sellers under about $1,268 a month in sales, because above that Pro costs less.

Skool's help center charges one bundled fee of 10% + 30c per transaction on all sales (https://help.skool.com/article/86-subscriptions-faq). It doesn't split out a platform cut, so the whole fee goes in native_processor and platform_fee_pct is 0. Payments must go through Skool's own merchant account ('you cannot use your existing Stripe account'), so you can't use your own processor; members, courses, videos and live calls are unlimited, and annual price = '2 months free' on the pricing page (monthly x 10/12).

Skool's help center charges one bundled fee of 2.9% + 30c for transactions up to $899 and 3.9% + 30c above $900 (https://help.skool.com/article/86-subscriptions-faq). No separate platform cut is stated, and you can't use your own Stripe because processing only runs through Skool's merchant account. Limits are unlimited, as on Hobby; annual price = '2 months free' on the pricing page (monthly x 10/12). Model uses 2.9% + 30c; sales above $900 cost 3.9% + 30c.
