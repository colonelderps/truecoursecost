Podia pricing and fees

No affiliate links: every vendor link goes straight to the platform's own pricing page. Disclosure.

Fees change; confirm on the vendor's pricing page before buying.

Calculate your annual cost

Podia's Mover plan costs $49 a month plus a 5% transaction fee, while Shaker ($99) and Earthquaker ($179) charge no Podia fee. Card processing is quoted at 2.9% + 30c on top. Mover suits sellers under about $1,000 a month in sales, because above that Shaker costs less.
